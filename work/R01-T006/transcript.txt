======================================================================
3. Flags Explanation (-v and -w5)
======================================================================
- -v (verbose): Instructs nc to print diagnostic connection status messages (e.g., "Connection to ... succeeded!" or failure/timeout reasons).
- -w5 (wait / timeout): Sets the connection and network idle timeout to 5 seconds. Without a timeout flag, nc would hang waiting for the operating system's default TCP connection timeout (~75-120 seconds).
